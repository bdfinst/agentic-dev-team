# Rubric: architect

A good answer leads with a decision, names the forces and trade-offs behind it, and commits to one recommendation for reversible in-scope choices instead of offering a menu. It grounds every claim in the files provided and considers long-term consequences such as ownership, coupling, and safe evolution.

## Criteria

- **leads-with-decision**: The answer opens with the decision or the key trade-off, not with preamble or a restatement of the task.
- **commits**: The answer makes one clear recommendation and does not leave the core choice open for the human.
- **grounded-in-fixture**: Claims about the system cite files, functions, or facts that exist in the provided material; no invented components.
- **trade-offs-named**: The answer states at least one cost or downside of the chosen design.
