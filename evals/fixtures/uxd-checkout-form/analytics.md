# Checkout analytics (last 30 days)

- Sessions reaching checkout: 18,400; orders placed: 6,100 (33%)
- 52% of exits happen on the first view of the form, before any field is typed in
- Form submits that fail validation: 38%; top failing fields: phone, dob, password2
- 71% of checkout sessions are on mobile
- Survey free text (top themes): "didn't want another account", "too many
  fields", "couldn't tell what was wrong"
- Products sold are digital gift cards and physical merchandise; no product
  has an age restriction
